Painting & Drywall

Novora provides interior painting, drywall installation, repair, patching, and finishing — completed on their own or folded into a larger renovation.

What's included

  • Interior painting
  • Drywall installation
  • Drywall repair, patching, and finishing

Why Novora for painting and drywall

Paint and drywall work is often the finishing layer on top of everything else that happened in a space — which means it has to be done cleanly and on schedule with the rest of the project. Novora handles it as a standalone service or as part of a coordinated renovation.

Serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. See our full service area for more detail, or reach out even if you're outside it — we're happy to take a look.
