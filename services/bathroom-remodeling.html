Bathroom Remodeling

Novora's bathroom remodeling services range from smaller improvements to complete renovations, balancing appearance, durability, functionality, and cost.

What's included in a bathroom remodel

  • Tubs, showers, and tile
  • Vanities, flooring, and lighting
  • Plumbing fixtures, drywall, and painting

Why Novora for your bathroom

Bathrooms combine plumbing, tile work, electrical, and finish carpentry in a small footprint, where mistakes are expensive to fix. Novora manages the full scope directly, so nothing falls through the gaps between trades.

Serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. See our full service area for more detail, or reach out even if you're outside it — we're happy to take a look.
