Whole-Home Renovation

For properties that need significant, multi-room work, Novora provides extensive renovation services spanning every trade involved — with one point of contact managing all of it.

What's covered in a whole-home renovation

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, drywall, and painting
  • Carpentry, electrical work, and plumbing work
  • Coordination across every trade involved, instead of you managing several unrelated contractors

One company, every trade

One of the biggest frustrations property owners run into is coordinating separate contractors for separate parts of the same project. Novora manages multiple aspects of a whole-home renovation under one roof, so you have a single point of contact for the entire scope.

Serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. See our full service area for more detail, or reach out even if you're outside it — we're happy to take a look.
