Ground-Up Construction

Novora builds residential homes from the ground up — coordinating every phase and trade from early planning through a finished, move-in-ready property.

What ground-up construction with Novora includes

  • Early planning and coordination across every phase of the build
  • Management of the trades required to take a project from site work to completion
  • Attention to construction costs, scheduling, and workmanship throughout
  • A finished home evaluated as a complete product — design, durability, appearance, and long-term value

Why build with Novora

Ground-up construction is a major part of where Novora is headed, and it separates us from companies that operate purely as remodeling contractors. Because we also work as real estate developers, we don't just think about finishing construction — we think about the home's design, functionality, durability, marketability, and long-term value as a complete product.

Serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. See our full service area for more detail, or reach out even if you're outside it — we're happy to take a look.
