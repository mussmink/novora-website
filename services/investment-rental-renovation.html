Investment & Rental Property Renovation

Renovating an investment property takes a different approach than renovating a personal residence. Every dollar spent should have a purpose.

How we approach investor and rental projects

  • Prioritizing improvements based on what actually affects value, durability, and tenant or buyer appeal
  • Selecting practical materials that balance cost, appearance, and durability
  • Applying a real estate lens — spending money where it creates value, not just spending money

Why investors and landlords work with Novora

Because Novora's background includes real estate and development, we understand that a rental or investment property needs to look good, withstand use, and still make financial sense. We help property owners prioritize improvements accordingly, instead of over-improving a property that doesn't need it.

Serving Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. See our full service area for more detail, or reach out even if you're outside it — we're happy to take a look.
