Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It covers novoragroupacq.com only — not Novora Construction Group's other business practices outside this site.

Information We Collect

When you submit the "Start Your Project" form, we collect what you choose to provide:

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • Property address or project location
  • Project type, description, and estimated budget
  • Desired start date
  • Photos of the property or project, if attached

We don't collect this information any other way on this site — there are no accounts, logins, or other forms.

How We Use It

Submitted information is used only to review your inquiry and follow up about your project. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone outside of operating Novora Construction Group.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on a few outside services to function. Each sees a limited slice of information as part of doing its job:

Web3FormsDelivers form submissions to our email inboxWhatever you enter into the form
hCaptchaConfirms you're not a bot before the form submitsStandard bot-detection signals (no form content)

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use its own cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. The third-party services above may use their own cookies or similar technology as part of their normal operation (for example, spam-detection by hCaptcha).

Data Retention

Form submissions arrive directly in Novora's email inbox and are retained there for as long as needed to evaluate and follow up on your project.

Your Choices

If you'd like a copy of the information you submitted, or would like it deleted, contact Novora directly using the phone number or email you'd typically use to reach us.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults seeking construction, renovation, or development services and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If how this site collects or handles information changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date above.

This policy describes the technical operation of this website in plain language. It isn't a substitute for legal advice — for compliance with specific state or federal privacy laws, Novora should have this reviewed by an attorney.
