Where We Work

Novora Construction Group serves Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs — with most of our work concentrated across Chester, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties.

Primary Service Counties

These are the three counties where we do the majority of our ground-up construction, renovation, and development work:

Philadelphia County

Philadelphia and the surrounding neighborhoods within the city.

Montgomery County

Including areas such as Norristown, King of Prussia, Ambler, Conshohocken, and Lansdale.

Chester County

Including areas such as West Chester, Downingtown, Exton, Phoenixville, and Coatesville.

Outside This Area?

If your property is outside our primary service counties, reach out anyway. We review every inquiry to determine whether a project is something we can accommodate — proximity to our core area is one factor among several, not an automatic disqualifier.

What We Do Across This Area

Everything from a single-room renovation to a home built from the ground up, plus our own residential development projects. See the full breakdown on our Services page.
