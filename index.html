Sheet A-02 / Who We Are

We're the ones doing the work.

Novora Construction Group was created from experience in both real estate and construction. Being involved with properties from both perspectives taught us something important: construction isn't just about completing a list of tasks — every decision affects a property's appearance, functionality, and value.

We operate across residential remodeling, investment property renovation, whole-home renovation, ground-up home construction, and residential development — serving homeowners, investors, landlords, property owners, and developers throughout Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs.

We aren't a broker lining up subcontractors and hoping it comes together. We're the ones doing the work — with ownership on site to see it happen firsthand.

That's also why our pricing looks the way it does: we run on high project volume and a fair, reasonable margin per job — not a small number of customers paying for our entire profit.

What working with Novora gets you

  • Competitive, transparent pricing
  • Quality workmanship, self-performed
  • Straightforward proposals and scopes
  • Direct access to company ownership
  • One point of contact for every trade involved
  • Efficient, hands-on project management
  • Practical recommendations, not upsells
  • A real estate lens on cost, value, and return

Sheet A-04 / Ownership & Pricing

Direct access to ownership.

There are no unnecessary layers of management, no being passed from person to person, and no runaround when you need an answer. Our owners personally visit and examine job sites — the same people responsible for Novora's reputation are accessible to the people trusting us with their homes.

We're not trying to get rich from one customer or one project. Our business model is high volume, low margin per job — competitive pricing, consistent project volume, and long-term relationships instead of maximum profit on a single contract.
